Blue Penguin Bakery allergy information.
All our cakes and pastries contain wheat, eggs and milk, unless the product sheet says otherwise.
Products with nuts: the chocolate celebration cake (hazelnuts), the almond croissant (almonds), the carrot cake (walnuts), the gluten-free chocolate brownie (almonds), the raspberry macarons (almonds) and the vegan banana bread (walnuts).
Nut-free products: the vanilla cupcakes and the lemon tart. They are baked on a separate day each week to avoid cross-contact with nuts.
Gluten-free products: the chocolate brownie and the raspberry macarons. They are made without wheat, but in a kitchen that also uses wheat flour.
Vegan products: the sourdough loaf, the rye bread and the banana bread.
Every product sheet lists its full ingredients and allergens. If you have a severe allergy, please ask our staff before you buy.
