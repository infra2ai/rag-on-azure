Blue Penguin Bakery baking workshops.
Croissant workshop: every second Saturday of the month, 9 am to 12 noon, at the Oerlikon shop.
The workshop costs 85 francs per person, including ingredients and the croissants you bake.
Groups are limited to 8 people. Book on our website at least one week ahead.
Private workshops for teams can be booked on request.
