Blue Penguin Bakery: how to keep your bakes fresh.
Cakes keep for 2 days in the fridge. Take them out 30 minutes before serving.
Croissants are best on the day you buy them. Warm them for 3 minutes at 180 degrees the next day.
Sourdough and rye bread keep for 3 days in a paper bag, or for 1 month in the freezer.
