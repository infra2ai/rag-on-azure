Blue Penguin Bakery catering for offices and events.
We cater for groups of 20 people or more.
Breakfast box: 12 francs per person, with a croissant, a mini muffin, fresh fruit and orange juice.
Lunch platter: 18 francs per person, with filled sourdough rolls, a salad and a dessert.
Catering must be ordered at least 3 working days ahead.
Delivery within 10 kilometres is free for catering orders over 200 francs.
