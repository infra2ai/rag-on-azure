Blue Penguin Bakery custom cakes.
Custom cakes must be ordered at least 5 days ahead, with a 50 percent deposit.
Our bakers can add a name or a short message on top for free. Photo prints on a cake cost 12 francs.
Custom cakes start at 45 francs for 8 people and 70 francs for 15 people.
Custom cakes can be picked up at either shop, Old Town or Oerlikon, or delivered.
Custom cakes cannot be returned.
